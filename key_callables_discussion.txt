## Key callables, Tasks 1 to 40

This discussion covers the 40 final task orchestrators of `buy_the_rumor_sell_the_news_draft.ipynb`, in notebook task order. Thirty-six are named `run_task_1` to `run_task_40`; the orchestrators of Tasks 29, 32, 33 and 39 are `run_full_pipeline_task_29`, `run_single_tag_task_32`, `run_teacher_only_task_33` and `run_portfolio_book_verification_task_39`.

Python implementation author: **CS Chirinda**.

Behaviour is established from each orchestrator's signature and documented contract, the docstrings and code of the stage callables it composes, and the notebook's declared constants; the paper, `latex_context.txt`, is the methodological standard. Internal helpers are accounted for inside their parent task rather than counted as additional callables.

Notation: stock $i$, session $t$, event $e$ on stock $i$ at session $t_0$, simple return $r_{i,t}$, SPY return $r_{m,t}$, rolling beta $\beta_{i,t}$, abnormal return $AR_{i,t}$, sentiment sign $s_e\in\lbrace-1,+1\rbrace$, size bucket $k\in\lbrace\mathrm{small},\mathrm{mid},\mathrm{large}\rbrace$, window $w\in\lbrace[-5,0],[0,0],[+1,+5],[+6,+20]\rbrace$, neutral baseline $b_{k,w}$, event tag $\tau$. The paper numbers no equations, so the four that recur are labelled here: (A) the abnormal return $AR_{i,t}=r_{i,t}-\beta_{i,t}r_{m,t}$; (B) the window cumulation $CAR_{e,w}=\sum_{j\in w}AR_{i,t_0+j}$; (C) the baseline $b_{k,w}$, the mean $CAR$ of neutral events in bucket $k$ over window $w$; (D) the adjusted estimator $s_e(CAR_{e,w}-b_{k,w})$.

Data boundary: the NewsWitch corpus behind every published count, drift and p-value is proprietary and is not held by this project. Published figures below are comparison targets that the code checks, not results this document reports. No orchestrator returns a published figure as a computed value; the arithmetic is verified on planted fixtures with known answers. Task numbers identify notebook cells: `run_full_pipeline_task_29` executes Tasks 1–28 as its 28 stages, and Tasks 30–40 consume their outputs.

### Task 1 — `run_task_1`

- **Inputs**: `frames` (the seven tables) or keyword `source_paths` (seven Parquet paths), exactly one of the two; `config` with `raw_input_schemas` and `verification_targets`; `output_dir`.
- **Process**: judges the schema, then key uniqueness, then the five referential-integrity assertions plus the one-to-one link between the label store and the retained corpus, then the declared verification targets. It halts on the first failing class.
- **Outputs**: `schema_audit`, `hashes`, `bundle` (a frozen `IngestBundle` carrying measured provenance), `audit_path`.
- **Transformation**: each table is read with PyArrow memory mapping or measured as supplied, fingerprinted once by SHA-256 of its primary key, and reduced to an audit record; the caller's frames are never mutated.
- **Research role**: guards the corpus counts of Section 3: "over the sample this amounts to more than 70 million raw articles. The 4.57 million retained here are the important ones", on "2,591 tradeable symbols".
- **Equations**: the audited targets:

$$
N_{\mathrm{raw}}\gt 7\times10^{7},\qquad N_{\mathrm{retained}}=4.57\times10^{6},\qquad N_{\mathrm{symbols}}=2{,}591.
$$

- **Implementation distinction**: `schema_audit.json` is written before `IngestHalt` is raised, so the failing check survives the process that found it.

### Task 2 — `run_task_2`

- **Inputs**: `frames` (the seven ingested tables), `config`, `output_dir`.
- **Process**: profiles every column, checks temporal and dimensional consistency, then applies the gates. The merged `quality_report.json` replaces the profile-only report of step 1.
- **Outputs**: `quality_report`, `heatmap`, `deviation_log` (paths), `report`, `gate_decisions`, `halt`, `proceed_to_task_3`.
- **Transformation**: column profiles (completeness, identifier uniqueness, enumerated-range validity, tail quantiles) and consistency checks (timestamps in the sample window, price dates on the calendar, beta-window density, tercile feasibility, top-1 source-domain share) become one gate decision.
- **Research role**: protects the inputs of Section 4.3, where $\beta_t$ is "a rolling 252-day OLS beta ... (minimum 126 observations, missing betas set to 1)" and buckets are formed "each year ... by dollar volume".
- **Equations**: the halting completeness gate on each key field $j$:

$$
c_j=1-\frac{n^{\mathrm{missing}}_j}{n}\ \ge\ 0.99 .
$$

- **Implementation distinction**: 0.99 is a declared default, not a paper figure, and is logged as a deviation. Only completeness, identifier uniqueness, range validity and Task 1's integrity assertions can halt; Task 29 stops the pipeline on `halt = True`.

### Task 3 — `run_task_3`

- **Inputs**: `study_config`, `raw_crawl_c`, `retained_articles_c`, `symbol_master`, `raw_prices_c`, `market_proxy_prices_c`, `output_dir`, `tokenize_fn` (the story-embedder tokenizer); optional `backfill_manifest`, `exchange_calendar`.
- **Process**: cleanse (five drop-or-fix rules), normalise, derive; each `_c` frame is written to Parquet and the derived article frame is frozen as a snapshot with its SHA-256.
- **Outputs**: `frames`, `calendar_cleansing_record`, `snapshot_path`, `snapshot_hash` and five log paths (cleansing, derivation, dropped identifiers, deviations, normalisation).
- **Transformation**: a disallowed HTTP status is dropped, robots non-compliance quarantined, a paywalled truncated body flagged, a non-English article removed, and a missing adjusted close retained as null and flagged. Domains are resolved on the public suffix list, tickers uppercased without venue, timestamps parsed in their declared zone to UTC.
- **Research role**: Section 3 states that "3.4% of articles reference symbols without price history ... and 9.7% of events are skipped for missing price windows", so a missing close is flagged, never imputed.
- **Equations**: the true dollar volume that conditions the size terciles:

$$
DV_{i,d}=P^{\mathrm{unadj}}_{i,d}\,V_{i,d}.
$$

- **Implementation distinction**: the adjusted-close dollar volume is carried only as a sensitivity companion. The backfill flag uses the vendor manifest or a declared default, recorded as such.

### Task 4 — `run_task_4`

- **Inputs**: `raw_crawl_c`, `exchange_calendar`, `output_dir`; keyword `retained_articles`.
- **Process**: URL deduplication, near-duplicate content collapsing, then trading-day assignment. Dropped records from both stages form one ledger with a stage flag.
- **Outputs**: `articles_deduped`, `articles_deduped_dropped`, `articles_with_trading_day`, `half_day_deviations`, `paths`, `article_join`.
- **Transformation**: canonical URLs keep the earliest first-publication instant (ties: ascending crawl id); a 64-bit SimHash with a Hamming threshold collapses near-duplicates, with emitted clusters capped by a maximum pairwise distance against single-link chaining; survivors receive a session.
- **Research role**: implements Section 4.2: "articles published after 16:00 New York time roll to the next trading day".
- **Equations**: with $d$ the New York local date and $h$ the wall-clock time of article $a$, and $\mathcal{S}$ the session set:

$$
\mathrm{day}(a)=\begin{cases}d,& d\in\mathcal{S}\ \wedge\ h\le 16{:}00\\ \min\lbrace s\in\mathcal{S}: s\gt d\rbrace,&\mathrm{otherwise,}\end{cases}\qquad H(x,y)=\mathrm{popcount}(x\oplus y).
$$

- **Implementation distinction**: a sensitivity path repeats the rule with each session's actual close; the rows where the two disagree are the persisted half-day deviations. With `retained_articles`, survivors that are not retained articles are counted, not assigned.

### Task 5 — `run_task_5`

- **Inputs**: `symbol_master_c`, `raw_prices_c`, `retained_articles_c`, `sample_window`, `output_dir`, `mention_window_days` (default 30).
- **Process**: builds the ticker lifecycle, resolves every article to a symbol, then accounts for coverage attrition; step 3 receives step 1's indexed master, so coverage flags are computed once.
- **Outputs**: `ticker_lifecycle`, `symbol_master_indexed`, `article_resolution`, `unresolved_rate`, `attrition_ledger`, `n_symbols_master`, `n_symbols_with_price_history`, `paths`.
- **Transformation**: lifecycle segments per (symbol, ticker) handle ticker reuse; an article resolves by printed ticker and date (current listing, uncontested former ticker, reused ticker), by mention counts in a ±30-day window under concurrent sharing, then by exact company name, else unresolved.
- **Research role**: measures the Section 3 cohort, "3.4% of articles reference symbols without price history (delistings skew small)".
- **Equations**: price history requires an in-sample close above one cent, and the shortfall is partitioned exactly:

$$
N_{\mathrm{master}}-N_{\mathrm{history}}=N_{\mathrm{never\ priced}}+N_{\mathrm{delisted\ before\ sample}}+N_{\mathrm{no\ usable\ close}}.
$$

- **Implementation distinction**: published counts (about 3,000 stocks, 2,591 symbols) are never returned as literals; equivalent quantities are computed from the supplied data.

### Task 6 — `run_task_6`

- **Inputs**: `exchange_calendar_c`, `sample_window`, `output_dir`.
- **Process**: builds the indexed calendar (canonical New York session check, zero-based rank, UTC offsets, open and close instants), then audits it.
- **Outputs**: a `CalendarTask6` object carrying `calendar_indexed`, the `shift` and session-gap primitives, and the attached audit.
- **Transformation**: declared dates become a strictly ranked session axis; a noncanonical date is flagged, not dropped.
- **Research role**: Section 4.2 makes the trading day a session, and Section 4.3 windows are counted in sessions; every window endpoint, rumor gap and story age downstream routes through this axis.
- **Equations**: the $O(1)$ shift on session ranks:

$$
\mathrm{shift}(t,j)=s_{\mathrm{rank}(t)+j},\qquad \mathrm{gap}(t_1,t_2)=\mathrm{rank}(t_2)-\mathrm{rank}(t_1).
$$

- **Implementation distinction**: the audit requires at least 21 sessions beyond each side of the sample window, no missing or duplicated session, and a UTC offset matching the timezone database on every row, so a constant offset across a daylight-saving change is reported.

### Task 7 — `run_task_7`

- **Inputs**: `prices`, `symbol_master`, `delisting_returns`, `events`, `output_dir`.
- **Process**: computes session returns, flags adjustment-factor jumps for review, integrates delisting returns, validates the panel, counts event attrition.
- **Outputs**: `prices_returns`, `attrition`, `deviation_log_path`, `split_review`, `split_review_path` (`split_review_task_7.csv`).
- **Transformation**: closes become simple returns from the previous available session; a missing or zero endpoint yields a missing return, never zero or a forward fill.
- **Research role**: supplies $r_t$ of Section 4.3 ("Let $r_t$ be the stock's daily return"), cumulated by simple summation, so the return is a ratio, not a logarithm.
- **Equations**: the return, with the delisting return added on the master's last price date $T_i$:

$$
r_{i,t}=\frac{P_{i,t}}{P_{i,t^-}}-1,\qquad r_{i,T_i}\leftarrow r_{i,T_i}+r^{\mathrm{delist}}_i .
$$

- **Implementation distinction**: a move with $\lvert r\rvert\gt0.95$ is split-confirmed when the adjustment-factor ratio exceeds 1.5; otherwise its row and flag are kept but the return is set missing (ruling of 2026-11-06). Every factor jump above 1.5 enters the manual-review table.

### Task 8 — `run_task_8`

- **Inputs**: `market_proxy_prices_c`, `exchange_calendar`, `returns_panel`, `output_dir`; optional `size_buckets`, `market_proxy_symbol` (SPY), `window` (252), `min_obs` (126).
- **Process**: builds the market return on the session grid, estimates rolling betas, audits them; writes exactly the market-return grid and the beta panel.
- **Outputs**: `market_returns`, `alignment_report`, `betas`, `beta_audit`, `output_paths`.
- **Transformation**: a missing proxy session yields a missing return on that session and the next, never a return spanning the gap; each symbol's paired returns become a beta per session.
- **Research role**: implements Section 4.3: "$\beta_t$ a rolling 252-day OLS beta of the stock against the S\&P 500, as of day $t$ (minimum 126 observations, missing betas set to 1)".
- **Equations**: over the last $n_t\le252$ paired observations $W_t$ ending at $t$ inclusive:

$$
\hat\beta_{i,t}=\begin{cases}\dfrac{\sum_{s\in W_t}(r_{i,s}-\bar r_i)(r_{m,s}-\bar r_m)}{\sum_{s\in W_t}(r_{m,s}-\bar r_m)^2},& n_t\ge126\\ 1,& n_t\lt126\ \mathrm{or\ market\ variance\ at\ the\ floor.}\end{cases}
$$

- **Implementation distinction**: the fallback is reproduced, not corrected; the audit reports its share, the count exactly at 126 observations and, with buckets, both by size bucket.

### Task 9 — `run_task_9`

- **Inputs**: `prices_returns`, `market_returns`, `betas`; keyword `output_dir`, `missing_threshold` (0.20), `identity_tolerance` ($10^{-9}$), `subsample_frac` (0.05), `random_state` (20261026).
- **Process**: computes and persists the abnormal-return panel, diagnoses missing cells, and verifies the decomposition identity.
- **Outputs**: `abnormal_returns`, `missingness`, `decomposition`, `summary_line`, `manifest`.
- **Transformation**: betas are left-joined on (symbol, session) and the market return by date; any missing input yields a missing $AR$, and a return row with no beta row is labelled `beta_absent`.
- **Research role**: implements equation (A), Section 4.3: "The abnormal return is $AR_t = r_t - \beta_t r_{m,t}$".
- **Equations**: (A) and the verified identity:

$$
AR_{i,t}=r_{i,t}-\beta_{i,t}r_{m,t},\qquad \max_{i,t}\left\lvert r_{i,t}-\left(AR_{i,t}+\beta_{i,t}r_{m,t}\right)\right\rvert\le10^{-9}.
$$

- **Implementation distinction**: the identity is checked on a seeded 5% subsample first, then on the whole panel; the manifest is written before any violation raises. Missingness is separated by reason, so a calendar defect is distinguishable from thin history.

### Task 10 — `run_task_10`

- **Inputs**: `prices` (`symbol_id`, `date`, `close_unadj`, `volume_shares`), `config` (`size_buckets`, `ingestion.sample_window`), `output_dir`.
- **Process**: resolves settings with provenance, computes daily and annual dollar volume and the partial-year fallback, assigns terciles, applies the observation floor and validates.
- **Outputs**: `dollar_volume_daily`, `dollar_volume_annual`, `size_bucket_lookup`, `size_buckets`, `validation`, `deviation_log`, `first_year_convention`, `bucket_regimes`, `settings`.
- **Transformation**: bucket year $y$ is ranked on year $y-1$ dollar volume, descending, ties by symbol; the top third is large, the next mid, the rest small. The first year is ranked on its own opening months.
- **Research role**: implements Section 4.3: "stocks are split each year into three equal-sized groups (small, mid, large) by dollar volume", point-in-time, so no contemporaneous volume enters the conditioning set.
- **Equations**: counting only days with both inputs present:

$$
DV_{i,y}=\sum_{d\in y}P^{\mathrm{unadj}}_{i,d}V_{i,d},\qquad k_{i,y}=\mathrm{tercile}\left(\mathrm{rank}\ DV_{i,y-1}\right).
$$

- **Implementation distinction**: a name with fewer than 20 effective days in $y-1$ inherits its $y-1$ bucket only when $y-2$ was sufficient (or $y-1$ is the first year); otherwise it is dropped with an attrition record. Each year's construction convention is returned, so it cannot be applied silently.

### Task 11 — `run_task_11`

- **Inputs**: `study_config`, `audit_sample` (hand-audited records with recorded responses), `output_dir`.
- **Process**: builds the frozen gate template, pins the decoding configuration, validates the prompt on the audit sample.
- **Outputs**: `prompt_version`, `status`, `gate_prompt_template`, `gate_decoding_config`, `prompt_validation_report`, `artifact_paths`.
- **Transformation**: blocks in fixed order (role, company context, the four decisions, the five sentiment strings with audit codes, the output contract), with exactly three call-time placeholders, become a versioned template with a digest over template and configuration.
- **Research role**: reconstructs the vendor gate of Section 3: an LLM "decides whether the article is genuinely related to the stock and important enough to keep, assigns a sentiment direction on five levels ..., and writes a summary". The prompt carries no date, price or directional cue.
- **Equations**: the decoding pins and the inclusive compliance floor:

$$
T=0,\qquad p_{\mathrm{top}}=1,\qquad n^{\mathrm{out}}_{\max}=250,\qquad \frac{n_{\mathrm{schema\ valid}}}{n}\ge0.99 .
$$

- **Implementation distinction**: the sentiment string set is a pinned deviation because the paper leaves it unresolvable. A collapse of sentiment to Neutral halts. A failed audit still persists every artefact.

### Task 12 — `run_task_12`

- **Inputs**: `study_config`, `title`, `body`, `output_dir`; optional `count_tokens` (defaults to a declared whitespace approximation).
- **Process**: builds the teacher template, the response schema and the decoding configuration, truncates the article suffix to the remaining budget, assembles the full prompt.
- **Outputs**: `prompt_template`, `schema`, `decoding_config`, `prompt`, `suffix`, `artifacts`.
- **Transformation**: role, taxonomy with mandatory disambiguation clauses, attribute definitions, four few-shot exemplars and the seven-field contract form a cached prefix; the article forms the varying suffix.
- **Research role**: implements Section 4.1: "A frozen prompt presents 17 event-tag definitions, five binary attribute definitions ... and four example articles ... with a strict JSON schema", including the two junk categories, price commentary and promotional content.
- **Equations**: the schema and the context budget:

$$
\mathrm{primary}\in\mathcal{T}_{17},\quad \mathrm{secondary}\in\mathcal{T}_{17}\cup\lbrace\mathrm{none}\rbrace,\quad \mathbf{a}\in\lbrace0,1\rbrace^5;\qquad T_{\mathrm{prefix}}+T_{\mathrm{suffix}}+T_{\mathrm{out}}\le T_{\mathrm{ctx}}.
$$

- **Implementation distinction**: the schema admits no other property. The look-ahead exclusions are stated in the prompt itself: the teacher classifies text and never forecasts.

### Task 13 — `run_task_13`

- **Inputs**: `retained_articles_c`, `config`, `teacher_client` (one attempt per call), `output_dir`; keyword `prompt_template`, `decoding_config` (Task 12's artefacts).
- **Process**: draws the bootstrap sample, labels it with the frozen teacher, audits the label store, builds the run configuration.
- **Outputs**: `sample_result`, `labeled_articles`, `validation`, `proceed_to_task_14`, `run_config`, `artifacts`.
- **Transformation**: a seeded draw without replacement becomes a store keyed by `article_id` (idempotent), with label source, hard-label confidence, stratum, model, prompt version and a timestamp per label.
- **Research role**: implements Section 4.1: "The teacher labeled 29,472 random articles" "at about \$0.0002 per article", and "Every article records its label source".
- **Equations**: the charge per successful label (never per retry) and the three-sigma share check:

$$
C=0.0002\,n_{\mathrm{usable}},\qquad \lvert\hat p_\tau-p^{\mathrm{ref}}_\tau\rvert\le3\sqrt{\frac{p^{\mathrm{ref}}_\tau(1-p^{\mathrm{ref}}_\tau)}{n_{\mathrm{usable}}}}.
$$

- **Implementation distinction**: proceeding requires size within tolerance, the usable floor, a reconciled per-label charge and a valid source domain; Task 29 halts on `proceed_to_task_14 = False`. Without a human gold set, no agreement figure is produced.

### Task 14 — `run_task_14`

- **Inputs**: `output_dir`, `backbone_config` (checkpoint keys or `ckpt_*` aliases), `checkpoint_paths`; optional `last_known_sha256`, `deviation_log`, `training_labels`.
- **Process**: validates the pinned backbone facts, inventories and counts parameters, hashes the checkpoint, enforces provenance, persists the audit, runs the pooling permutation test, computes class weights, persists the untrained checkpoint.
- **Outputs**: `audit`, `n_params`, `audit_path`, `audit_sha256`, `checkpoint_path`, `checkpoint_sha256`, `class_weights`, `input_contract`, `provenance`, `pooling_permutation_check`, `canonical_facts_filled`.
- **Transformation**: declared shapes become an audited seven-head architecture: six layers, 768 hidden units, twelve heads of width 64, feed-forward 3,072, 514 positions, 52,265 tokens.
- **Research role**: implements Section 4.1: "a distilroberta-base student (82M parameters, seven heads: primary tag, secondary tag, five attributes)".
- **Equations**: the masked mean pool, inverse-frequency weights ($N$ labels, $K$ classes) and the DEF20 objective with $\lambda_1=1.0$, $\lambda_2=0.5$:

$$
\mathbf{h}=\frac{\sum_j m_j\mathbf{H}_j}{\sum_j m_j},\qquad w_c=\frac{N}{K\,n_c},\qquad \mathcal{L}=\lambda_1\mathrm{CE}_w^{(17)}+\lambda_2\mathrm{CE}_w^{(18)}+\sum_{a=1}^{5}\mathrm{BCE}_a .
$$

- **Implementation distinction**: this task instantiates no torch model; the persisted checkpoint is the declared state, and real training happens in Task 15. A changed checkpoint hash halts unless the deviation log accepts it.

### Task 15 — `run_task_15`

- **Inputs**: `config` (`encode`, `decode`, `output_dir`, `model` or the label vectors); optional `bootstrap_labels`, `articles`, `tag_names`, `model` (a `torch.nn.Module`), `output_dir`.
- **Process**: assembles inputs from Task 13's labels, builds each article input, splits an internal hold-out, computes class weights (report: all labels; objective: training split), trains, persists weights, checkpoint and report.
- **Outputs**: the training report: per-epoch records, best epoch and metric, chance level, halt flags, both class-weight vectors, the split and its provenance, hyperparameters, precision plan, counters, checkpoint record.
- **Transformation**: title, a space and the body truncated to 512 tokens (a 416-token head plus 96 tokens of numeral-bearing tail sentences) become gradient updates of the injected student.
- **Research role**: implements the student training of Section 4.1 under the stated mechanics: AdamW, linear warmup and decay, clipping, mixed precision, early stopping on macro-F1, numeral-preserving truncation.
- **Equations**: early stopping on held-out macro-F1, with a 6% linear warmup then linear decay to zero:

$$
\mathrm{F1}_{\mathrm{macro}}=\frac1K\sum_{c=1}^{K}\frac{2P_cR_c}{P_c+R_c},\qquad \eta_u=\eta_0\min\left(\frac{u}{0.06U},\ \frac{U-u}{0.94U}\right),\ \eta_0=2\times10^{-5}.
$$

- **Implementation distinction**: batch 32, weight decay 0.01, at most five epochs, patience two, gradient norm 1.0, bf16 with a logged fp32 fallback, logits clamped at $\lvert10\rvert$. The internal 10% hold-out is not the paper's 10,000-article set (Task 17). A non-finite loss or a collapse below chance halts after the report is written.

### Task 16 — `run_task_16`

- **Inputs**: `retained_articles_c`, `bootstrap_article_ids`, `student_model`, `teacher_call`, `tag_names`, `output_dir`, `labeled_articles_path`; keyword `prompt_version`, `n_draw` (600,000), `n_select` (100,000), `quota_per_tag` (500), `batch_size` (50,000), `seed` (42), `cost_usd` (0.0002), `max_attempts` (3).
- **Process**: first-pass inference over a fresh pool, quota-first uncertainty selection, routing of the selection to the teacher.
- **Outputs**: `pool_rows`, `sample_size`, `artifacts` (pool and delta paths), with the stage results.
- **Transformation**: the pool excludes bootstrap identifiers; the 500 least-confident rows of every tag are taken first, then the remainder fills in ascending confidence to exactly 100,000; validated teacher labels are appended to the store.
- **Research role**: implements Section 4.1: "trained on these labeled 600,000 fresh articles; the 100,000 lowest-confidence articles went back to the teacher (uncertainty sampling, with a quota for rare tags)".
- **Equations**: the confidence and the usable band:

$$
\mathrm{conf}(x)=\max_{c}\ p_c(x),\qquad 99{,}000\le n_{\mathrm{usable}}\le100{,}000 .
$$

- **Implementation distinction**: a tag short of its quota records a deficit and takes nothing from other tags. Throughput is reported against the paper's 125–160 articles per second.

### Task 17 — `run_task_17`

- **Inputs**: `bootstrap_labels`, `active_learning_labels`, `v1_checkpoint`, `model`, `encode`, `pool_table`, `gold_table`, `output_dir`; optional `max_tokens` (512), `numeral_budget` (96), `sample_size` (10,000), `seed`, `heldout_evaluation`; keyword `articles`, `training_overrides`, `predict_fn`, `teacher_call`, `prompt_version`, `teacher_max_attempts`.
- **Process**: merges the labels with provenance, warm-starts from v1 and writes v2, draws the disjoint dual-label sample, measures agreement against the teacher and the human gold sample, verifies the bands, writes the report.
- **Outputs**: `merged_rows`, `n_dual`, `checkpoint`, `agreement`, `human_gold`, `label_provenance`, `human_anchor`, `dual_label_construction`, three artefact paths.
- **Transformation**: two label frames become one 129,463-row training set and a retrained student; a held-out sample labelled by both becomes agreement statistics.
- **Research role**: implements Section 4.1: "the student was retrained on the merged 129,463 labels (29,472 + 99,991)", agreeing "on 87.5% of primary tags overall and 94.0% at confidence $\ge 0.8$ (85% of articles); attribute agreement is 92--99%".
- **Equations**: primary agreement and its high-confidence restriction, with bands $0.875\pm0.03$ and $0.94\pm0.02$:

$$
A=\frac1n\sum_{x}\mathbb{1}\left[\hat y^{S}(x)=\hat y^{T}(x)\right],\qquad A_{0.8}=\frac{\sum_x\mathbb{1}[\mathrm{conf}(x)\ge0.8]\,\mathbb{1}[\hat y^{S}=\hat y^{T}]}{\sum_x\mathbb{1}[\mathrm{conf}(x)\ge0.8]}.
$$

- **Implementation distinction**: agreement with the teacher is a pseudo-oracle, so the human gold sample is evaluated separately as the only absolute check. Per-tag precision and recall bound later false-discovery bands.

### Task 18 — `run_task_18`

- **Inputs**: `articles`, `cached_teacher`, `predict_fn`, `teacher_client`, `contract`, `out_root`; keyword `active_learning_labels`.
- **Process**: batched student inference over every article, the routing merge, cardinality validation, then re-read and derivation audits.
- **Outputs**: `n_student`, `n_final`, `student_store`, `label_store`, `labeled_articles`, `cache_assembly`, `provenance_store`, `validation`, `persisted_student_store`, `derivation_audit`, `ledger`, `student_confidence_by_tag`, `checkpoint`, `provenance`.
- **Transformation**: student rows become a deduplicated 16-column store in which each article has exactly one label and one recorded source.
- **Research role**: implements Section 4.1: "Deployment uses the classifier everywhere, routes the 15% low-confidence tail back to the teacher, and keeps teacher labels where they exist ... \$156.29 in total".
- **Equations**: with the contract's threshold $\theta$ (the paper's 15% tail corresponds to $\theta=0.8$):

$$
\mathrm{label}(x)=\begin{cases}\mathrm{cached\ teacher},& x\in\mathcal{C}\\ \mathrm{routed\ teacher},& x\notin\mathcal{C}\ \wedge\ \mathrm{conf}(x)\lt\theta\\ \mathrm{student},&\mathrm{otherwise.}\end{cases}
$$

- **Implementation distinction**: a cached label short-circuits routing, so no article is charged twice. The routing rate must lie in $[0.10,0.20]$; the teacher-call reconciliation passes within 500 calls and halts beyond 10,000. Every student label is re-derived from its persisted head vector.

### Task 19 — `run_task_19`

- **Inputs**: `articles`, `out_dir`; optional `encoder`, `model_name`, `fit_sample_size` (500,000), `eigenvalue_floor` ($10^{-6}$), `n_pairs`, `n_probes`, `n_neighbors`, `seed`; keyword `url_source`, `labels`.
- **Process**: encodes every article, fits the whitening on a (year, tag)-stratified sample, persists the parameters, applies the persisted transform to all articles, audits the geometry.
- **Outputs**: `paths` (raw store, parameters, whitened store, validation, deviation log), `validation`, `n_articles`, `n_fit`, `embedding_dim`, `fit_sample_strata`, `deviation_log`.
- **Transformation**: title, a space and the summary are encoded and L2-normalised, centred, whitened with a full untruncated matrix, and normalised again.
- **Research role**: serves Section 4.2's clustering "on the cosine similarity of their title-and-summary embeddings"; whitening corrects the anisotropy that otherwise makes a cosine threshold uninterpretable.
